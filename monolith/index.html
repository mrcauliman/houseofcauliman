What MONOLITH is

A living public record where each post is a small constrained tile, tied to a public wallet identity. It is simple by design. Constraint keeps the monument clean.

How it works

  • Choose a small logo or artwork tile within strict limits
  • Sign a small XRP payment to post
  • Your tile is placed on the wall and linked to your public wallet identity
  • The wall grows forever, visibility stays controlled by time and relevance

Rules of MONOLITH

MONOLITH is a monument. It stays clean by law.

  • One tile per post, constrained format only
  • No edits after posting
  • No deletions, ever
  • No full banners, no wall-sized ads
  • No wallet connection, no keys, no custody
  • Public identity only, public data only

Endless without chaos

  • New posts are most visible, older posts fade into the archive
  • Zoom out to see the monument, zoom in to inspect marks
  • History remains accessible, the default view stays clean

    The record is endless. Visibility is controlled by time, zoom, and archive behavior.
  • Why pay to post
    To kill spam and keep MONOLITH clean. The wall is a record, not a dumpster.
  • Is this art or utility
