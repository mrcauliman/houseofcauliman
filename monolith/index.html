How it works

MONOLITH is a forever wall on the XRPL. Each tile is a coordinate, like a plot on a map.

Coordinates use ORIGIN at 0,0. North is N-1, N-2. South is S-1, S-2. East is E-1, E-2. West is W-1, W-2.

Owning a tile ties it to your wallet. Your logo, image, and note become your permanent mark.

Companies can buy multiple tiles to build billboard blocks. Premium lanes and special numbers can be reserved.
