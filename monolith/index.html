MONOLITH is not a gallery. It is a wall. You claim a tile, post a constrained logo or artwork, and the ledger becomes the receipt. Years from now, people will look up the wall and see who was real.

What it is Simple by design
  • A permanent wall made of tiles, each tile is a constrained slot for a mark
  • Marks are logos, tags, or artwork that stay inside the tile boundaries
  • Each placement is backed by on-chain proof, not vibes and screenshots
  • The wall is designed to stay readable and legendary, even at scale

If you were real back then, you are on MONOLITH. That is the entire game.
