What it is
A permanent wall where each coordinate is a tile. Claim a tile with your mark and your legacy note.
  • One tile. One mark.
  • No edits. No deletes.
  • XRPL only.

How tiles work
North, South, East, West. Numbers increase as you move away from ORIGIN.
  • ORIGIN is the center anchor.
  • N-1 is one tile north. S-1 is one tile south.
  • E-1 is one tile east. W-1 is one tile west.
