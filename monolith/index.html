MONOLITH

The Wall of Record. An on-chain monument of proof and presence. Leave your mark. History keeps it.

What it is

MONOLITH is an on-chain graffiti wall. One tile equals one constrained mark. No edits. No deletes.

  • A permanent wall made of tiles, each tile is a constrained slot for a mark
  • Every tile is time-stamped proof that you were present
  • Years from now, people check MONOLITH to see who was real
  • If you were real back then, you are on MONOLITH.
