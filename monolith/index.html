How it works

MONOLITH is a permanent wall of tiles on the XRPL. Each tile is a coordinate, like a plot of land on a map. Claim later links a wallet, a logo or image, and a note. Forever.

Coordinate model
  • ORIGIN is dead center at 0,0
  • N-1 north, S-1 south
  • E-1 east, W-1 west
  • Diagonal shows both, like N-3 / E-7
Search formats
  • N-11 / E-23 or N-11/E-23
  • 23,-11
  • Legacy input works, like E23-N11
Why it matters
  • Tiles connect to wallets. Your plot of land on the XRPL
  • Companies can buy blocks to make billboards
  • Premium lanes and special numbers can be reserved
  • Pricing is in $XRP. USD cost moves with $XRP
