XRPL Safety

First Rule

If someone asks for your seed phrase or private key, they are taking your wallet.

Scams

  • Fake support
  • Phishing links
  • Fake tokens
  • Impersonators

Red Flags

  • Guaranteed returns
  • Urgency pressure
  • Random DMs

People To Avoid

  • Fantasy number accounts
  • Engagement farmers
  • No-risk narratives

Safety Moves

  • Use burner wallet
  • Test small first
  • Verify links
  • Slow down decisions
