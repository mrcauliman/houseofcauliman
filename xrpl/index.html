Why This Matters

Most people are sitting around watching a bag and waiting for price to save them. That is not how real value gets built.

The iPhone did not become what it became because people stared at the hardware. It became dominant because the network, the software, the apps, and the utility stack made it part of everyday life.

XRPL is the same kind of opportunity. $XRP is the rail. XRPL is the network. The value comes from people building on top of it, using it, and creating real tools that people actually touch.

This section exists to teach people how the ledger works, how to use it, how not to get burned, and why building apps and utilities on the rail is the real move.

That is the point. Not sitting still. Not cheering from the sidelines. Building, using, and making the network more valuable through real activity.
