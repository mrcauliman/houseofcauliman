What People Get Wrong

Most people look at price first and skip usage completely.

That is backwards.

Usage creates demand. Demand is what gives the system weight.

Think of It Like the iPhone

The iPhone did not become powerful just because it existed.

It became powerful because people used it every day for real things.

  • Messages
  • Apps
  • Payments
  • Work
  • Entertainment

The more useful it became, the more valuable the whole ecosystem became.

XRPL works the same way.

What Usage Looks Like on XRPL

  • Sending value instantly across the world
  • Trading assets on the built-in DEX
  • Providing liquidity to pools
  • Using apps built on the ledger
  • Moving between currencies
  • Settlement between systems

Where $XRP Fits

$XRP is the rail that moves value.

It can act as a bridge between assets, currencies, and systems.

When value moves through the system, $XRP can be part of that flow.

Adoption Is Not a Switch

Adoption does not happen all at once.

It builds over time.

  • More users
  • More apps
  • More liquidity
  • More transactions

Each layer strengthens the system.

Utilities and Apps Matter

Most people do not use ledgers directly.

They use apps and utilities built on top of them.

The better the apps, the easier the usage.

The easier the usage, the more adoption grows.

Bottom Line

No usage means no sustained value.

More usage means more demand pressure on the system.

That is why usage and adoption matter so much.
