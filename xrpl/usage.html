What Usage Actually Means

Usage is not people talking about a chain online. Usage is people signing transactions, buying assets, moving funds, listing products, and interacting with the ledger directly.

That means swaps, order books, NFT buys, direct settlements, and wallet-based execution.

DEX

The XRPL DEX is the order book layer. Buyers and sellers place orders. The ledger matches them.

  • Order book based
  • Good for precise pricing
  • Requires active orders and real liquidity
  • Best when you want more control over the entry

AMM

The AMM is the pool layer. You are swapping against liquidity in a pool instead of matching directly with another order.

  • Instant swap flow
  • Price moves with trade size
  • Slippage matters
  • Thin pools move harder and punish size faster

Buying Tokens

Most token buyers are doing one of two things. They are hitting the order book or swapping through an AMM. Xaman usually handles the signing flow directly.

  • Check the issuer
  • Check available liquidity
  • Understand whether you are using DEX or AMM
  • Sign only after you know what you are buying

Buying NFTs

XRPL NFTs are usually bought through a marketplace or a direct project listing. Same ledger underneath. Different front door.

  • Marketplace listing gives cleaner discovery
  • Direct site listing gives the project more control
  • Xaman signs the final transaction either way
  • You still verify what collection and item you are buying

OTC

OTC means over the counter. Direct deal. Wallet to wallet. No marketplace protection. No pool logic. No order book in the middle.

  • Useful for private deals and bulk settlements
  • Trust risk is much higher
  • You are relying on the counterparty
  • Bad OTC habits get people burned fast

Direct Site Listings

Some projects sell directly through their own site. That can still route to the same ledger actions under the hood. The website is the interface. XRPL is still doing the settlement.

  • Project controls the buying flow
  • Can feel cleaner for users
  • Still needs wallet signing
  • Still needs issuer and asset verification

Reality

Every sale type has strengths and weaknesses. DEX gives precision. AMM gives convenience. OTC gives flexibility. Marketplaces give cleaner discovery. Direct sites give projects more control.

None of that removes the need to verify what you are signing.
