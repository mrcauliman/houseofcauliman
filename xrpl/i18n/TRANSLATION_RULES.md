# Translation rules

English in `catalogs/en.json` is the source. One JSON object, the same keys, UTF-8, no comments.

Keep these strings exact whenever they appear, including inside a sentence:

- `$XRP`
- `XRPL`
- `XRPL LAYMAN`
- `Xaman`
- `Testnet`
- `Mainnet`
- `AUGUR`
- `House of Cauliman`
- `NFTokenID`
- `NFTokenPage`
- `TakerGets`
- `TakerPays`
- `0.2`
- every `{placeholder}` such as `{s1}`, `{br1}`, `{objects}`, `{pct}`, `{fee}`, `{mult}`, `{checked}`, `{total}`
- example addresses that start with `r`

Do not add new facts about the XRP Ledger. Do not change numbers. Do not turn the reserve example into a different base reserve. Freeze does not mean destroy. A trust line is not an endorsement. A seed cannot be reset. The standard transaction cost is destroyed, not paid to validators. MPTs are a different token standard from trust-line tokens; do not merge those ideas.

Product and field names that stay in Latin: NFT, AMM, DEX, URI, MPT, USD, TOKEN, CLUB, Issuer A, Member B, Record N.
