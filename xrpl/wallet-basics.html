XRPL Wallet Basics

What a Wallet Is

A wallet controls access to your assets on the ledger. It controls keys. That is it.

What You Can Share

Your public address. That is safe.

What You Never Share

  • Seed phrase
  • Private key
  • Recovery words

Hot vs Cold

Hot wallet is active and convenient.

Cold wallet is for protection and storage.

Basic Flow

  • Create wallet
  • Back up seed phrase
  • Test small
  • Then scale
