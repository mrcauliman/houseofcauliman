What NEB Is

NEB is designed to watch the network itself instead of waiting for charts to tell the story after the move already happened.

The job is simple: detect trading opportunities, validate the setup, and execute under rules.

What NEB Watches

  • Wallet activity
  • Liquidity movement
  • Order book behavior
  • Market momentum
  • Exchange conditions across XRPL markets

What NEB Does

  • Scans XRPL market activity
  • Surfaces tradeable conditions
  • Validates setups before action
  • Executes under controlled logic
  • Logs behavior for review and refinement

Build Status

Why It Matters

Most people react after the move. NEB is built to watch the network itself.

It exists to turn raw XRPL activity into actual trading behavior.

AUGUR reads the ledger. NEB trades the ledger.
