What It Is

Automated trading infrastructure concept for XRPL markets.

What It Watches

Wallets, liquidity, order books, AMMs, routes, and ledger movement.

What It Does

Scans for tradeable conditions and logs what should be reviewed next.

Product Rules

  • Non-custodial by design
  • No profit promises
  • Scanner before paper trading
  • Paper trading before live execution
  • Risk controls before scale

Build Path

  • Phase 1: static interface and scanner shape
  • Phase 2: XRPL feed reader
  • Phase 3: opportunity journal
  • Phase 4: paper trading
  • Phase 5: controlled execution after proof

System Narrative

AUGUR reads the ledger. NEB trades the ledger later, only after scanner logic, paper trading, risk rails, and execution controls are proven.
