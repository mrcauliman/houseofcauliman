NEB

Network Edge Bot

NEB is an automated trading bot for the XRP Ledger.

Its purpose is simple: detect market inefficiencies and capture edge automatically.

The bot scans XRPL order books, liquidity pools, and routing paths looking for spreads, mispriced liquidity, and execution opportunities.

What NEB Does

  • Scans XRPL order books for spread opportunities
  • Identifies liquidity inefficiencies across trading pairs
  • Executes automated trades when profitable conditions appear
  • Operates without custody of user funds

Build Roadmap

  • Market scanning engine
  • Paper trading environment
  • Strategy testing and safety controls
  • Controlled live trading deployment

Why It Exists

Crypto markets constantly produce small inefficiencies. NEB is designed to detect those edges and capture them automatically.

It is infrastructure — not a hype product.
