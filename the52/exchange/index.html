How it will work

Four easy steps, once it opens

1

Sign in

Sign in on this website with Xaman, using the wallet that holds your card.

2

See your card

You’ll see your old card and the new card waiting for you. You can do one or two cards at a time.

3

Approve in Xaman

Xaman shows you the swap. Check it, then approve it in your own wallet.

4

Done

Once the XRP Ledger confirms it, your old card is back with House of Cauliman and your new card is in your wallet.

Old card first, always. Your new card is only released when the XRP Ledger confirms your old card came back. The best way does both in one step — either both happen, or neither does — so you can’t end up with nothing.

Questions

Common questions

When does the swap open?

Soon. We’re finishing final checks. We’ll announce it on official channels, and this page will switch on.

Does it cost anything?

The new card is free. The XRP Ledger charges a tiny network fee, and your wallet may briefly need a small XRP reserve during the swap.

Do I need to do anything now?

No. Just keep your original card in the same wallet.

I sold or moved my card. What now?

When the swap opens, cards that moved are checked one by one. Please don’t try to fix it with a transfer.

What about my holder perks?

Holder access follows the card you hold, so it moves to your new card. Files you already downloaded stay yours.

How do I know it’s really you?

The swap only happens on houseofcauliman.com. We never ask for your secret or seed, and never ask you to send a card through a direct message.
