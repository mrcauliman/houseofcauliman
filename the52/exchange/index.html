Who can exchange

Eligible holders

Subscribers who received an allocated original Card 05 or Card 06, and who still hold that exact original in the same registered wallet.

Each original unlocks only its own assigned corrected card. The receiving wallet cannot be changed on this page. If a card has moved, or anything does not match the allocation record, it goes to individual review — never an automatic change.

What is not included

Outside this exchange

Return before replacement

Your original comes back first. Always.

The corrected card is released only against a returned original. The exchange is designed so a replacement can never be issued while the original is still outstanding.

1 · Check

Your original, its assigned replacement and your wallet are checked against the allocation record and the ledger.

2 · Return

Your original is returned to the House issuer account.

3 · Validate

The return must be confirmed in a validated XRP Ledger result before anything else happens.

4 · Replace

Only then is your corrected card delivered to the same wallet.

Preferred method: the return and the delivery are packaged into a single all-or-nothing ledger transaction, so either both happen or neither does. Where that is not possible, the original must be validated as returned before any replacement offer is created.

Safeguards

Built to protect holders

Exact recipient

The corrected card can only go to the wallet that holds the matching original. Recipients cannot be edited.

One exchange per card

Each original and each corrected card can be used once. Duplicate or repeated requests are refused.

No guessing

If a result is uncertain, the exchange is paused for manual reconciliation. Please do not sign again or repeat a transfer.

Verified only

Nothing your browser sends is trusted on its own. Every step is checked against the allocation record and the ledger.

Before you exchange

Good to know

Does the exchange cost anything?

The corrected card is free. Standard XRP Ledger network fees apply, and temporary offers can briefly require account reserve. A failed ledger transaction can still use its network fee.

What happens to access linked to my original card?

Holder access follows ownership. Your access moves with the card you hold. Files you have already downloaded remain yours.

Should I sell or move my original now?

If you want to exchange, keep your original in the wallet that received it. A card that has moved needs individual review.

How will I know it is official?

The exchange will only ever run on houseofcauliman.com. We will never ask for your secret or seed, and we will never ask you to send an NFT to anyone through a direct message.

I see a problem with my card or wallet.

Do not try to fix it with a transfer. When the exchange opens, any mismatch is routed to individual review.
