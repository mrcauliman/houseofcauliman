THE 52 is a year-long XRPL collectible series from House of Cauliman. Fifty-two original characters, each built around the personalities, habits, and archetypes found throughout crypto culture. One new character is released each week, creating a complete 52-card collection across the year.

Eligible House of Cauliman 𝕏 subscribers with a registered wallet receive each weekly release while their subscription is active. Every edition is recorded on XRPL, and select cards may unlock holder experiences and utility across the House of Cauliman ecosystem.

How to find and accept your NFTs

  1. Open Xaman and select the wallet you registered for THE 52.
  2. Go to Events → Requests and open the incoming NFT offer.
  3. Check that it is the correct THE 52 NFT with an offer amount of 0 XRP, then accept it.
  4. Once acceptance is validated on the ledger, the NFT is in your wallet and should appear under NFTs.

Seeing “validated” in All/history? That may be the offer being created, not your acceptance. Check Requests for an offer you can act on.

The offer is 0 XRP; normal network fees and XRPL reserve requirements still apply.

No offer showing? Connect your registered wallet here, open the relevant released card, and check Subscriber Delivery, or use Subscriber Delivery Help. Do not sign unfamiliar requests.
