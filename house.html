What the House Is

The House of Cauliman is the governing structure behind the ecosystem.

It exists above individual tokens, collections, and products. Nothing operates independently. Everything routes through the House.

This is coordination by design, not coincidence.

Why the House Exists

Most ecosystems fracture over time. Too many narratives. Too many decision points. No center of gravity.

The House exists to maintain coherence. To enforce standards. To prevent structural decay as participation increases.

Growth without structure is temporary.

What the House Controls

  • Liquidity strategy and reinforcement
  • Supply timing and release structure
  • Access permissions and eligibility
  • IP direction and expansion
  • Product sequencing and deployment order

Control is centralized to preserve long-term alignment.

Who the House Is For

  • Long-term participants
  • Builders who value standards
  • Collectors who understand access
  • People aligned with structure over reaction

The House is not designed for constant churn.

Closing

The House does not promise outcomes. It builds conditions.

Participation is optional. Alignment is required.
