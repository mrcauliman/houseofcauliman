XRPL Investing Reality

This is the truth filter. Liquidity, slippage, market cap confusion, and how price actually moves on XRPL.

This is where people get it wrong. Price does not move based on belief. It moves based on liquidity and execution.

Liquidity

Liquidity determines how easily price moves. Thin liquidity moves fast. Deep liquidity absorbs size.

Slippage

Large orders move price against you. That is slippage. Most new users underestimate this.

Market Cap Myth

Market cap is not cash. It does not mean that amount can be withdrawn.

Market Reality

Price moves where liquidity allows it to move. Not where people want it to go.
