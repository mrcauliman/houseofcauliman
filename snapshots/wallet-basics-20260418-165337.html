What a Wallet Actually Is

A wallet is your access point to the ledger. It does not magically store money inside an app like a game inventory. It controls access to the assets tied to your public address.

If you control the keys, you control the wallet.

If somebody else controls the keys, you are using their system.

Wallet app on one side. Public address on the ledger. Keys are the control layer in the middle.

Hot Wallet vs Cold Wallet

A hot wallet is connected to the internet and used for daily activity. A cold wallet is kept offline and used for storage.

Hot Wallet

Fast. Convenient. Used for signing, trading, minting, and interacting with apps.

  • Best for active use
  • More exposed
  • Xaman fits here

Cold Wallet

Slower. More secure. Built for storage and long-term protection.

  • Best for larger holdings
  • Offline by design
  • Better for long-term storage
Hot wallets are for activity. Cold wallets are for storage. Use both correctly.

Exchange vs Wallet

An exchange gives you access through a company login. A wallet gives you direct control through your keys.

People confuse access with ownership all the time. They are not the same thing.

  • Exchange holds the funds for you
  • Wallet puts you in control
  • If you do not control the keys, you do not control the funds
Exchange equals custodial access. Wallet equals self-custody and direct ledger control.

On-Ramp vs Off-Ramp

An on-ramp converts dollars into crypto. An off-ramp converts crypto back into dollars.

Most people on-ramp through an exchange, move their $XRP into a wallet, then use XRPL from there.

  • On-ramp: USD to $XRP
  • Off-ramp: $XRP to USD
  • Wallet is where you take control after purchase
Bank to exchange to wallet to XRPL. Reverse the flow when exiting.

Xaman is the execution wallet people need to understand first on XRPL.

What You Can Share

Your public address is shareable. That is what people use to send assets to you.

Your secret, seed, recovery phrase, and signing control never get shared. Not with support. Not with a friend. Not with a website. Not with anybody.

What Never Gets Shared

Secret / Seed

This is the control layer. Whoever has it can control the wallet.

Recovery Phrase

Same rule. This is not for screenshots, cloud notes, emails, or messages.

Custody in Plain English

Custody means who actually controls the assets.

  • Exchange custody means the company controls the funds
  • Self-custody means you control the wallet
  • Self-custody comes with responsibility. Lose the keys and that is on you

Reality Check

Convenience usually means more trust in somebody else. Security usually means more responsibility on your side.

Use the right tool for the right job. Hot wallet for activity. Cold wallet for storage. Exchange for entry and exit. Wallet for control.
