$XRP

First Principle

$XRP does not become more valuable because people yell bigger numbers.

Usage is what matters. Real movement of value. Real settlement. Real adoption.

How Price Actually Moves

Price does not move in a permanent vertical line.

Usage builds. Price steps up. Sellers take profit. Price pulls back. Usage builds again. The next step forms.

That is closer to reality than fantasy moonshot thinking.

What Fantasy Numbers Ignore

Extreme price targets imply extreme system-wide alignment, liquidity, and usage. That is not a casual overnight event.

Big numbers require big rails. Big rails require real integration and time.

What To Watch Instead

  • Usage growth
  • Liquidity depth
  • Transaction activity
  • Adoption by real systems
  • Whether the network is being used, not just discussed

Bad Investor Habits

  • Buying narratives without understanding mechanics
  • Following fantasy accounts
  • Assuming every rally is permanent
  • Ignoring risk and liquidity
  • Thinking timeline and destination are the same thing
