Overview

A continuity system for digital assets

Most people assume saving a seed phrase is enough. Real life proves otherwise. Family members often do not know what wallets exist, what app to use, what chain holds the assets, or how to move safely without making a mistake. LAV organizes that chaos into a calm, clear process.

Wallet clarity

Record what exists, what it is used for, and what app or device is needed to access it.

Trusted roles

Keep the heir, executor, attorney, or trusted helper tied to the plan in one place.

Family packet

Produce a simple emergency packet that helps the right person move carefully and correctly.

Why it matters

Self-custody still needs continuity

Holding your own assets is only part of the job. If the people you trust most cannot understand what you left behind, access can still be lost. LAV is built to reduce confusion, reduce avoidable errors, and give families a better path forward.

For the owner

Create a cleaner record of wallets, contacts, recovery guidance, and packet-ready instructions.

For the family

Provide a calm, readable path that does not assume crypto knowledge when pressure is already high.
