What LAV Covers

One system, multiple layers

Wallet records

Organize the wallets, networks, and access paths that matter so the next person understands what they are looking at.

Trusted roles

Define who should know what, who should act first, and who should only be involved at the right point.

Family packet

Create a guided handoff layer that gives direction, sequence, and safer starting context.

Release structure

Control how access unfolds so action happens in order instead of all at once.

Heir guidance

Present the experience in a way that helps a non-expert move carefully under pressure.

Audit visibility

Keep track of reviews, updates, and meaningful changes so the system stays current.

Why it matters

What LAV is trying to prevent

Assets getting stuck

Families can be left with real value but no clear path for understanding what exists or what to do first.

Bad first decisions

Pressure and confusion create mistakes. LAV is structured to slow that down and improve order.

Scam exposure

Unclear systems create room for bad advice and rushed trust. LAV is meant to reduce that risk.
