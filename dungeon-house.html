The Game

A new dungeon-crawler experience built from the ground up.

Dungeon House carries the raw replay energy of classic arcade dungeon crawlers while building its own world, combat systems, enemies, relics, heroes, floors, and progression. The goal is simple. Make the game fun first. Make the House world deep enough to keep growing.

Choose Your Hero

Select your character before the descent. Each class will bring its own strengths, combat rhythm, abilities, and survival strategy.

Descend Deeper

Move through dangerous floors filled with enemies, keys, doors, traps, hidden routes, treasure, and escalating pressure.

Build Your Power

Fight for weapons, armor, equipment, potions, spells, relics, and treasure earned inside the dungeon.

Survive the Swarm

Enemy pressure will climb as the dungeon grows deeper. Movement, timing, positioning, and resource decisions matter.

Fight the Bosses

Major rooms and floor milestones will test the gear, skill, and decisions that carried the run forward.

Return Stronger

Score, progression, relic collection, equipment depth, and future challenge modes will give each return real purpose.

Free Trial Dungeon

  • Character selection
  • Combat and enemy encounters
  • Treasure and equipment

  • Spells and abilities
  • Final Trial Dungeon encounter

Full Campaign Unlock

After the free Trial Dungeon, players will be able to unlock the full campaign through a one-time $XRP purchase. No chain transactions during combat. No pay-to-win nonsense. The dungeon loot stays earned through gameplay.

Build Direction

The foundation is being built now.

The first objective is the playable spine. Movement. Dungeon collision. Combat. Enemy pressure. Health. Treasure. Doors. Exit conditions. Once the core feels right, Dungeon House expands into character classes, deeper floors, bosses, controller support, local co-op architecture, progression systems, score chasing, and the full campaign.
